Phyllospadix japonicus, known as Asian surfgrass,[2] is a species of flowering plant in the family Zosteraceae.[3][4] It is found along the seacoasts of Japan, Korea, and China (Hebei, Liaoning, Shandong). It grows in the intertidal zone along the shore.[5]

Phyllospadix japonicus is a perennial herb that spreads by means of rhizomes. Its leaves are long, reaching up to 100 cm, though they are rarely more than 2.5 mm wide.[6][7][8][9][10][11]
