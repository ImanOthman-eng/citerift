Lithocarpus kunstleri is a species of flowering plant in the family Fagaceae. It is a tree endemic to Peninsular Malaysia.[2] It is native to primary and secondary lowland rain forest and swamps up to 300 meters elevation. It is threatened by habitat loss.[1]

The species was first described as Quercus kunstleri by Joseph Dalton Hooker in 1888. In 1931 Aimée Antoinette Camus placed the species in genus Lithocarpus as L. kunstleri.[2]
